# Sound library credits and licences

The Studio's built-in sound library (`assets/sounds/`) contains recordings made by other people and released under
**Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: they are dedicated to the public domain and can be used, changed and
distributed for any purpose, commercial or not, without asking permission. The full legal text is in
[CC0-1.0.txt](CC0-1.0.txt). Kenney's own licence file is included as [Kenney-License.txt](Kenney-License.txt).

Credit isn't required by CC0, but we gladly give it. Thank you to everyone below.

Each sound was converted to 22,050 Hz mono 16-bit WAV. Some were shortened (long ambience), faded at the ends
(so loops restart without a click) and levelled (one-shots peak at 90 %, loops at 75 %). Those changes are
also released under CC0.

Games you make with Adventure Creator can include any of these sounds with no obligations.
